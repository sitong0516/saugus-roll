About Saugus Roll

Saugus Roll imagines a bright, welcoming place to switch off the screen, lace up a pair of skates, and spend time with other people.

A modern rink with a community spirit

The concept combines the playful colors of classic roller rinks with a clean, modern design. Its imagined activities include beginner sessions, music nights, and small-group celebrations. These are design ideas for this class project, not advertised services at a real business.

Room for every kind of skater

Start somewhere

The first lap can be the hardest. A welcoming rink gives new skaters room to build confidence.

Find your rhythm

Music, color, and movement make the rink a place for creative expression.

Share the floor

Patience and respect help people with different abilities enjoy the same space.
