Famous Quotes

Five famous literary quotes about trying, caring, and being yourself. They were not written about skating, but their themes fit the spirit of the rink.

Courage and confidence

"Our doubts are traitors, and make us lose the good we oft might win, by fearing to attempt."

— William Shakespeare, Measure for Measure, Act 1, Scene 4

"Trust thyself: every heart vibrates to that iron string."

— Ralph Waldo Emerson, Self-Reliance

Enthusiasm and self-expression

"Nothing great was ever achieved without enthusiasm."

— Ralph Waldo Emerson, Circles

"In short, all good things are wild and free."

— Henry David Thoreau, Walking

"I am large, I contain multitudes."

— Walt Whitman, Song of Myself, section 51
